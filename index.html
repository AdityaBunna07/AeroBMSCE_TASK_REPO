The mission

Curiosity has a runway here.

From first sketches to flight testing, our members learn by making. We create a space for students to turn technical knowledge into aircraft and UAV systems that perform.

Our vision is to become a leading aerospace and unmanned systems research group known for design excellence, innovation and competitive success.
