From early designs in the lab to international flight lines, these are the milestones that define AeroBMSCE.

2026

International flight history

At SAE International Aero Design West in Fort Worth, Team Yaksha placed 2nd internationally in the MATLAB Simulation Awards and ranked #16 globally in the Micro Class. The team also won 1st place for Unique Technology Development at SAE Aerothon ADDC.

2025

A season of podiums

2nd place overall at SAE Aerothon (₹1,25,000); 1st place for Best Technical Presentation at SAE Drone Design Challenge; and 1st place for Best Innovation at SAE Autonomous Drone Design Challenge.

2024

Speed and precision

2nd place at NITK Drone Racing, 1st place at Global Academy of Technology Drone Racing, and 12th place globally at SAE International Aero Design West.

2022

Nationally recognised

3rd place at SAE Aerothon, 2nd place at the South India Exhibition, 4th nationally at AI-VTOL, and top global positions in design report and technical presentation at SAE Aero Design West.

2020 / 2018

A legacy of competition

Team Blue Impulse won SAE India nationally in 2020, alongside an 8th-place global design standing at SAE Aero Design West. In 2018, the team placed 3rd overall and 2nd in Design Report at SAE India.
